\title{Algebraic and topological invariant fields for cryptic-pocket detection\\
on the official CryptoBench receptor-only benchmark}
\author{Shane \and Xuening Wu}
\date{July 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We evaluate deterministic, training-free geometric detectors on the task of
recovering cryptic binding-site residues from apo receptors, using the official
CryptoBench test fold (MMseqs2 clustering at 10\% sequence identity,
cluster-disjoint). The detectors have no trained weights and no fitted
hyperparameters, and were never exposed to any CryptoBench partition; the input
contract is receptor coordinates only, enforced at runtime by a ligand-leak
guard that rejects any non-solvent heteroatom. On \NUnits{} single-chain
evaluation units a rigid geometric detector with no training of any kind attains
a per-residue ROC-AUC of \GeoAuc{} (95\% CI \GeoAucCI{}), while a locally
executed P2Rank 2.5.1 baseline, scored on its own residue-level output, reaches
\PtwoRAuc{} \PtwoRAucCI{}.

Between the two we place a detector whose entire inference path is table lookups
and integer addition. \NTabWires{} wires --- \(43\) local quantities of the apo
receptor, each read under five neighbourhood statistics --- are banded into
quaternary digits against each chain's own order statistics, and
\NTabTables{} tables of \TabWidth{} digits each hold two integers per address,
counted on the cluster-disjoint CryptoBench training fold: how many residues
landed in the cell and how many of those were cryptic. The score is the sum of
those cell ratios with integer fan-out in \([-\TabCap{}, \TabCap{}]\), followed
by one neighbourhood mean. This field attains ROC-AUC \TabAuc{} \TabAucCI{},
PR-AUC \TabPr{} \TabPrCI{}, MCC \TabMcc{} \TabMccCI{} and F1 \TabFOne{}
\TabFOneCI{}, ahead of P2Rank on all four. One difference is resolved by this
fold: F1, by \TabFOneD{} \TabFOneDCI{} at \(p=\TabFOneP{}\). The differences on
ROC-AUC (\TabAucD{} \TabAucDCI{}) and PR-AUC (\TabPrD{} \TabPrDCI{}) contain
zero. MCC is ahead by \TabMccD{} \TabMccDCI{}, an interval that excludes zero at
our pre-registered resampling seed and does not do so under most others, and we
report it as unresolved for the reason given below. We claim a resolved
advantage on F1 and parity on the other three, and nothing stronger.

The ROC-AUC margin deserves less than that. Over this fold the standard error of
a mean ROC-AUC is \FoldSE{}, so \TabAucD{} is \LeadInSE{} of one standard error,
and it is the largest of \NArchOnFold{} architectures of ours that have been
scored on these same units --- a count taken from an artifact ledger rather than
from memory, after a hand-written version of this disclosure proved wrong. A
maximum over that many correlated estimates is biased upward by about the size
of the margin, so we treat the ROC-AUC ordering as carrying no information and
ask the reader to do the same.

MCC is the one that needs saying out loud, because two defensible choices decide
it and only one of them is honest. Each paired difference is taken on the
structures where both methods have a value, which for MCC is \TabMccNPaired{} of
\NUnits{}, since MCC is undefined wherever a confusion matrix is degenerate and
P2Rank has six such structures here while the table field has none; differencing
each method's mean over its own coverage instead would fold ``which structures
were scorable'' into a number read as ``which method is better''. On the shared
set the two MCCs are \TabMccMatched{} and \TabMccMatchedBase{}, and at our
pre-registered resampling seed that difference excludes zero. It does not
survive the seed: across \NSeedProbes{} seeds the MCC interval excludes zero in
\TabMccSeedSig{} of them, so the exclusion is a property of the draw and not of
the fold. F1 excludes zero in \TabFOneSeedSig{} of \NSeedProbes{}, and ROC-AUC
and PR-AUC in none. MCC is therefore reported as unresolved.

Two design facts carry that result and both are quantitative. First, cell
occupancy: a width-six quaternary table has \(4^{6}\) cells and holds about fifty
training residues per cell, so its fraction carries a relative standard error
near 60\%, while a width-two table has sixteen cells and about
\TabCellOccupancy{} residues in each. Second, coverage: \TabWidth{}-digit tables
see only pairs, so a single partition of the wires reads a vanishing fraction of
the pair lattice, and \TabRounds{} independent partitions are needed before the
bank is competitive. A previous version of this manuscript concluded from a
single-partition experiment that the interactions available to a quaternary code
do not pay for their variance; that conclusion was an artifact of coverage and is
withdrawn. With sufficient coverage and a ridge of \TabRidge{} on the scatter of
the table outputs, the counting field is ahead of a first-order functional of the
same wires on all four metrics rather than behind it.

Selection discipline is reported in full rather than summarised. Wire families,
table width, pool size, regulariser, fan-out cap, gate radius and gate weight
were all ranked on a cluster-disjoint half of the training fold, where the frozen
configuration measured \TabPickAuc{}. The test fold was read \NTestReads{} times
during development, at \ReadAucI{}, \ReadAucII{} and the value above, with
paired differences against P2Rank of \ReadDeltaI{}, \ReadDeltaII{} and
\TabAucD{}; each change between readings was diagnosed and made on the training
fold, and all three readings are reported so that the reader can see how much of
the final figure is architecture and how much is repetition. Three
deformation-based ablations (isotropic breathing, anisotropic ANM shear,
ultrametric-gated shear) score at or below the rigid detector on every metric and
are reported as falsified rather than omitted. All numbers are regenerated by a
fail-closed pipeline with per-file SHA-256 verification and explicit
denominators. We claim no binding affinity and no clinical or design utility
(\texttt{clinical\_grade=false}).
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}
This paper makes three claims about the official CryptoBench receptor-only test
fold. First, a detector whose inference is table lookups and integer addition
over quaternary digits of receptor geometry is ahead of a locally executed P2Rank
on all four official metrics, and the F1 difference is resolved by this fold
while the other three are not. Second, the quantity that governs such a detector
is not the width of its tables but the occupancy of their cells and the coverage
of the digit pairs: narrow tables read many times beat wide tables read once, by
a margin this fold does resolve. Third, restricting the resolution path to
counting costs nothing relative to a first-order functional of the same wires,
which is the opposite of what we reported previously and is explained in
Section~\ref{sec:results}.

Explicitly not claimed: a resolved advantage over P2Rank on ROC-AUC, PR-AUC or
MCC; parity on any other benchmark; binding affinity; candidate efficacy; drug
design utility; clinical readiness; or global novelty. The field's cells and its
integer fan-out are compiled on the CryptoBench training fold, so it is
\emph{not} training-free; the training-free detectors are reported separately and
are weaker. The fan-out comes from one regularised closed-form solve, which is a
real-valued object at compile time even though inference is integral, and the
paper says so wherever the field is described. Appendix~\ref{app:gf4} is an
algebraic negative control that is excluded from these claims and from every
generalization statement.

\section{Data}
The benchmark is the official CryptoBench test fold (Skrhak et al.,
\emph{Bioinformatics} 41(1):btae745, 2025; dataset \url{https://osf.io/pz4a9/}),
whose splits are cluster-disjoint under MMseqs2 at 10\% sequence identity.
Receptors are materialized from RCSB and every receptor and label file is pinned
by SHA-256 in a manifest; the loader refuses to run if the manifest is absent,
if the declared clustering method or identity threshold differs, if any hash
mismatches, or if a cluster identifier appears in more than one split. There is
no fallback path: a smaller pilot subset cannot be substituted for this fold.

The evaluation unit is a \((\text{pdb},\text{chain})\) pair, not a PDB entry:
192 units span 190 distinct entries because two entries contribute two chains
each. A further 38 fold units are multi-chain assemblies and are excluded before
evaluation, enumerated in the manifest, because the residue indexing used here is
chain-agnostic and cannot address such assemblies unambiguously. The exclusion is
recorded rather than silent.

\section{Methods}
\paragraph{Input contract.} Every detector receives receptor coordinates only.
A decorator asserts, at call time, that the file carries no non-solvent HETATM
record and no non-polymer ATOM record; a violation is recorded as a crash, not
skipped. An import-graph test additionally forbids any detector module from
importing the scorer, so a label cannot reach a predictor through code.

\paragraph{Rigid geometric detector.} Free grid points outside the van der Waals
surface are scored by a buriedness functional, the fraction of probe directions
blocked by an atom within a ray cylinder. Candidate centres are the most buried
points, deduplicated by a minimum separation. There are no trained weights and no
fitted hyperparameters; the geometric constants are fixed a priori and are not
tuned against any CryptoBench partition.

\paragraph{Deformation ablations.} Two ablations test whether admitting
deformation improves recovery of pockets that are closed in the apo frame. The
isotropic ablation applies a fixed set of radial dilations. The anisotropic
ablation replaces isotropic dilation with the receptor's own low-frequency,
non-rigid modes from an anisotropic network model: a candidate is promoted when a
small shear opens a large still-enclosed cavity at that site. Shear amplitudes
are fixed by the spectrum as \(c_k=\Delta x\sqrt{\lambda_1/\lambda_k}\), so no
scalar is tuned. The eigen-decomposition is pinned for determinism by a fixed
Lanczos start vector, zero convergence tolerance, and an eigenvector phase
convention; repeated runs are byte-identical.

\paragraph{Three classes of detector, and what ``training-free'' means here.}
The detectors above see no CryptoBench partition of any kind: their constants are
fixed a priori and the apo receptor is their only input. The field detectors
below are not of that class and we do not describe them as training-free. They
are compiled on the official CryptoBench training fold, which is cluster-disjoint
from the test fold at 10\% sequence identity under the same MMseqs2 partition.
Among them we draw one further distinction, and it is mechanical rather than
rhetorical. The counting fields store only pairs of integers --- how many
residues addressed a cell, and how many of them were cryptic --- and every
quantity they read at inference time is a ratio of two such integers; no gradient
step is taken, no real-valued weight is solved for, and no continuous
discriminant is fitted. The linear field relaxes exactly that last property: it
solves one regularised symmetric system for a real coefficient vector. It still
takes no gradient step, runs no iteration, differentiates nothing, and reads no
test residue, but it is a fitted model and every table in this paper labels it as
one. We separate the three classes rather than averaging over them, because
averaging over what a method was allowed to see is what makes such tables
misleading.

\paragraph{Quaternary resolution field.} Six geometric invariants per residue are
banded into quaternary digits against the residue's own chain quartiles, which
is a comparator network over the chain and carries no constant between chains.
The digit word addresses a dense table of \(4^{6}\) cells holding the two
integer counters. The addressable width is not free: with \(N\) training residues
at cryptic base rate \(r\), a cell holds \(rN/4^{d}\) expected positives, and
requiring at least one gives \(d \le \log_4(rN)\). On this fold
\(rN = 13\,524\) and the bound is \(d \le 6.87\), so a flat word may not exceed
seven digits. An earlier ten-digit variant violated it: \(4^{10}\) cells against
\(13\,524\) positives left 98.9\% of the address space undrivable.

\paragraph{Algebraic counting field.} To reach the descriptive richness of a
feature-based predictor without leaving exact quantities, the word is widened to
35 invariants, each a closed-form algebraic or integer topological function of
the receptor: solid-angle occlusion and protrusion; the Laplacian spectrum of the
induced neighbourhood graph together with the Betti numbers of the local pocket
lining; the closed-form least-squares gradient and discrete Laplacian of the
packing-density field; non-Archimedean ball geometry and the residue valuation;
inertia-tensor anisotropy, planarity and sphericity; and depth, coordination and
contact order. Because the capacity bound forbids addressing 35 digits at once,
the invariants are partitioned into six thematic blocks of at most six digits,
each block compiled into its own dense table, and the blocks fused by addition of
their cell fractions. Fusing rather than cascading is deliberate: a block table
estimates its fraction from about 57 residues, whose standard error is 53\% of
the base rate, and banding such an estimate into two bits is an irreversible
decision taken on a noise-dominated statistic. Measured on this fold, every
cascaded topology we compiled scored below the flat control, while parallel
fusion scored above it. Block multiplicities in the sum are small integers given
by the rank of each block's own compiled Gini index, i.e.\ fan-out replication of
a table, not a real coefficient. A final spatial gate adds, at five radii, the
mean of the fused score over the closed geometric neighbourhood, which states
combinationally the fact that a cryptic site is a contiguous patch of residues
rather than an isolated one.

\paragraph{Wire expansion.} The linear field widens the word from the 35
invariants to \NLinWires{} wires without introducing any learned representation.
Seven published per-residue constants are appended (Kyte--Doolittle hydropathy,
side-chain volume, aromatic ring count, formal charge at pH 7, hydrogen-bond
donor and acceptor counts, and rotatable side-chain bonds), together with one
counted propensity \(P(\text{cryptic}\mid\text{residue type})\) compiled on the
training fold alone and carried into the artifact, so no test residue ever
contributes a count to it. Each of the resulting 43 quantities is then taken both
at the residue and as its mean over the 6, 14 and 20\,\AA{} neighbourhoods, which
states at three scales the fact that a cryptic site is a property of a
neighbourhood rather than of one residue. Quantization is unchanged: each wire is
banded into a quaternary digit against its own chain's order statistics.

\paragraph{Five statistics of a neighbourhood.} The table field takes the wire
expansion one step further, and the step is the one that matters for its result.
Each of the 43 local quantities is read not only at the residue and as its
neighbourhood mean, but under five statistics of the same fixed geometric
adjacency \(N_r(i)=\{j:\lVert c_i-c_j\rVert\le r\}\):
\[
C_r[x]_i=\frac{1}{|N_r|}\!\!\sum_{j\in N_r(i)}\!\! x_j,\quad
\mathrm{sd}_r[x]_i=\sqrt{C_r[x^2]_i-C_r[x]_i^2},\quad
D_r[x]_i=x_i-C_r[x]_i,\quad
K_r[x]_i=\frac{|\{j\in N_r(i):x_j<x_i\}|}{|N_r(i)|},
\]
with the mean at \(r\in\{6,10,14,20,26\}\) and the other three at
\(r\in\{6,14,20\}\), for \(43\times 15=\NTabWires{}\) wires. None of the four is
a kernel, a bandwidth or a fitted constant; each is an unweighted statistic of a
ball. The second moment states how uniform a neighbourhood is, which the mean
cannot: two residues can share a mean hydrophobicity because every neighbour is
moderately oily or because half are oily and half are charged, and a cryptic site
sits at the interface between such regions. The centred difference is a discrete
Laplacian, large where a residue is unlike its surroundings; as a real number it
is a difference of two wires already present and adds nothing to a linear
functional, but the field does not read real numbers --- each wire is separately
ranked within its chain and cut into a quaternary digit, and the digit of a
difference is not a function of the digits of its terms. The local rank is the
only one of the four that is not a moment: it is invariant to any monotone
rescaling of the wire, and it separates a residue that is marginally the most
hydrophobic of its neighbours from one that is marginally the least, which
\(D_r\) reports as two small numbers of opposite sign and comparable magnitude.

\paragraph{The table field.} \NTabTables{} tables are formed as \TabRounds{}
independent random partitions of the \NTabWires{} wires into groups of
\TabWidth{}, so each wire appears exactly \TabRounds{} times, each time with
different partners, and coverage of the digit-pair lattice comes from the number
of rounds rather than from the width of any table. Each table stores
\(\mathrm{tot}_k[a]\) and \(\mathrm{pos}_k[a]\) per address, counted on the
training fold; an address no training residue reached takes the fold's base rate,
which is the only value that adds no information, and there are
\NTabCellsEmpty{} such cells out of \NTabCells{}. The score is
\[
S(i)=\sum_{k} m_k\,\frac{\mathrm{pos}_k[a_k(i)]}{\mathrm{tot}_k[a_k(i)]},
\qquad m_k\in\mathbb{Z}\cap[-\TabCap{},\TabCap{}],
\qquad
F(i)=S(i)+\TabGateWeight{}\,\bar{S}_{\TabGateRadius{}}(i)\,
\frac{\mathrm{sd}(S)}{\mathrm{sd}(\bar S_{\TabGateRadius{}})},
\]
so inference is one cell read per table, an integer-weighted sum, and one
neighbourhood mean. \NTabUsed{} tables carry a non-zero fan-out and the total is
\TabFanOut{}. The fan-out is obtained by rounding the solution of one symmetric
system in the table outputs, regularised by \(\TabRidge{}\) of its trace, onto the
integer grid. That is a real-valued object at compile time and we label it as
such: what is integral is the inference path, not the compilation. The gate is
matched to the standard deviation of \(S\) rather than to its maximum, because
the maximum of a score field over a chain is an order statistic of a handful of
residues and a max-normalised gate therefore mixes in a different amount on every
structure. The operating point for MCC and F1 is the per-chain top
\(\TabOperatingQ{}\) fraction, fixed on the training fold.

\paragraph{Linear readout, and why table width is the free variable.} The readout
is one regularised solve, \(w=(A^{\top}A+\lambda\,\bar{\sigma}I)^{-1}A^{\top}t\),
over the standardised digits, with \(\lambda=\LinRidge{}\) and \(t\) the centred
label. The score is then \(s+\LinGateWeight{}\,\bar{s}\), where \(\bar{s}\) is
the mean of \(s\) over the closed \LinGateRadius{}\,\AA{} neighbourhood rescaled
to the spread of \(s\) --- the same patch statement the counting field makes,
applied to a continuous field.

Which readout to use is not a matter of taste here, and the quantity that decides
it is the width of the table. A width-\(d\) quaternary table has \(4^{d}\) cells,
so on \(N=\NTrainResidues{}\) training residues at base rate \TrainRate{} a cell
holds \(N/4^{d}\) residues and its fraction has relative standard error
\(\sqrt{(1-r)/(rN/4^{d})}\). At \(d=6\) that is about 60\%: the bank models the
joint distribution inside each window exactly, but each exact statement is about
a noisy quantity. At \(d=2\) it is about 3\%, and there are only \(\binom{K}{2}\)
such tables over the top \(K\) wires, few enough to combine by the same single
solve. A first-order functional sits at the other end, representing no
interaction at all but estimating each coefficient from the whole fold. We
therefore compiled all three and let the training fold choose, which is reported
in Section~\ref{sec:readout} rather than assumed here.

\paragraph{Metrics.} All four metrics are computed over the same residue universe
(every residue of the apo chain) at the same operating point (residue belongs to
any predicted pocket): ROC-AUC, average precision (PR-AUC), MCC and F1.
Uncertainty is a paired bootstrap over evaluation units, 10{,}000 resamples, 95\%
percentile CI, with the same resample indices applied to every method so that
between-method correlation is preserved. A metric that is undefined for a
structure is null and is dropped from that metric's denominator; it is never
imputed as zero. Per-unit values are keyed on \((\text{pdb},\text{chain})\), and a
colliding key raises rather than overwriting a row.

\paragraph{Baselines.} P2Rank 2.5.1 is executed locally with a pinned version and
is a scored baseline. A uniform bounding-box sampler provides a chance floor.
PocketMiner is reported as data-unavailable: the published artifact is a trained
model binary with no per-residue predictions, so no paired interval against it is
computed and no value is imputed. Tool unavailability is never counted as a miss.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
The official evaluation emits \NRows{} telemetry rows, \NUnits{} evaluation
units times \NMethods{} methods, with per-residue metrics available on all of
them and no silent drops. Every method returns a prediction on
\NUnits{}/\NUnits{} units.

P2Rank is scored on the residue-level table it natively emits
(\texttt{*\_residues.csv}): the calibrated \texttt{probability} column as the
continuous per-residue score, and its own \texttt{pocket > 0} assignment as the
binary operating point. An earlier revision of this harness instead reconstructed
a residue signal from pocket centres, keeping the top 5 pockets and weighting
every residue within 6\,\AA{} of a centre by \(1/\text{rank}\). That is not
P2Rank's prediction and it is lossy: \(1/\text{rank}\) is a five-valued staircase
that cannot resolve the residue ordering P2Rank emits, and truncation ties every
remaining residue at zero. Under that harness P2Rank measured ROC-AUC 0.621;
scored on its own output it reaches \PtwoRAuc{}. We report the corrected figure.

Whether that constitutes reproducing the published baseline is a question about
aggregation, and it is answered by measurement rather than assertion.
CryptoBench pools over every residue in the subset; this paper takes the mean
over structures, because a paired test needs one value per structure and because
pooling lets a single 900-residue protein outvote forty small ones. Recomputed
under the published convention -- pooled, P2Rank's own probability column, its
own pocket assignment as the operating point -- five of the seven published
quantities return within 0.03: AUC \(0.800\) against \(0.81\), AUPRC \(0.236\)
against \(0.21\), accuracy \(0.857\) against \(0.85\), FPR \(0.124\) against
\(0.14\), MCC \(0.275\) against \(0.27\). TPR is \(0.545\) against \(0.62\); the
subsets are not identical, since the published row is CB-P2RANK-apo and we
evaluate the \NUnits{} single-chain units of the 222 apo structures after
excluding 38 multi-chain assemblies with ambiguous residue numbering, and recall
is where a subset difference shows first.

The published F1 of \(0.81\) is not reproduced under any convention we can
compute: positive-class F1 is \(0.303\), the class-weighted average \(0.885\),
the macro average \(0.612\). On a task with a \(5.7\,\%\) positive rate a
class-weighted F1 is dominated by the negatives and a value near \(0.81\) is
attainable by predicting almost nothing, so we neither reproduce that column nor
compare against it. Every F1 in this paper is the positive-class F1.

\paragraph{The architecture was not chosen on the test fold.} \NCandidates{}
fusion architectures were compared during development. Selecting among them by
test-fold score and then reporting the winner's test score would make the
reported figure the maximum of \NCandidates{} noisy estimates. The choice was
therefore made on the training fold alone, split into two halves with disjoint
MMseqs2 clusters (\NSelectFitUnits{} units to compile on, \NSelectPickUnits{} to
rank on), and the official test fold is read once, for the single architecture
that procedure names. It names ``\SelectedArch{}'' at ROC-AUC
\SelectedArchAuc{} on the held-out training half, which is the architecture
reported here; the training-only ranking of all \NCandidates{} candidates agrees
with the test-fold ranking, so the test figure can be read as an out-of-sample
estimate rather than a selected maximum.

\paragraph{What limits the counting field.} Holding the estimator fixed and
widening the invariant word from six to \NAlgFeatures{} moves a closed-form
linear functional of the same invariants from ROC-AUC \FisherSix{} to \FisherAll{} on this
fold, so the descriptors carry the signal. Pushed through counter tables the
same invariants reach \AlgAuc{}. The gap is a capacity limit rather than a
descriptive one: a table over \(d\) quaternary digits has \(4^{d}\) free cells
and is bounded by \(d \le \log_4(rN) = 6.87\) here, whereas a linear form over
\(M\) rank features is determined by \(\Theta(M^{2})\) quantities, which for
\(M=\NAlgFeatures{}\) is \(1\,225\) against \(13\,524\) available positives. The
linear form can therefore address all \NAlgFeatures{} dimensions at once while no
admissible table can address more than seven, and unweighted or
integer-weighted partitions of the invariants do not close that gap.

That bound is about one table and it remains correct; what does not follow from it
is the conclusion we drew, that the counting construction has a ceiling below the
linear form. The bound limits how many digits one address may carry, not how many
addresses a bank may read. A bank of \NTabTables{} two-digit tables commits
\(\TabWidth{} \times \NTabTables{}\) digit slots and spends
\(\NTabCells{} - \NTabCellsEmpty{}\) occupied cells, each estimated from
\TabCellOccupancy{} residues, so it addresses all \NTabWires{} wires while no
individual address exceeds two --- and it measures above the linear form on all
four metrics (Section~\ref{sec:coverage}). The capacity theorem and the
measurement agree; our reading of the theorem did not.

\subsection{Choosing the readout on the training fold}
\label{sec:readout}
The readout was chosen by the same discipline as the fusion architecture and on
the same cluster-disjoint halves of the training fold: compile on
\NSelectFitUnits{} units, rank on \NSelectPickUnits{} units whose clusters the
first half never saw, and read the test fold once for the winner. On that
held-out training half the integer-multiplicity table bank reaches \PickCount{},
a linear functional of the \NLinWires{} first-order digits reaches
\PickLinDigits{}, and the same functional over the digit \emph{indicators} ---
which can represent a non-monotone response to a wire, at four times the
parameter count --- reaches \PickLinOnehot{}, slightly worse. A linear
combination of the bank's own cell fractions reaches only \PickLinTables{}: the
tables lose more to cell variance than they gain from modelling the joint
distribution, and no way of weighting them recovers it.

That diagnosis predicts where an interaction model should live, so we tested it
directly by varying the table width. Width-two tables over the top \(K\) wires
have sixteen cells each, estimated to about 3\% rather than 60\%, and there are
few enough of them to combine by the same solve. Their best configuration,
\PickPairBestName{}, reaches \PickPairBest{} --- above the width-six bank, below
the first-order functional, and monotonically worse as \(K\) grows. We concluded
from that sweep that pairwise interactions among quaternary-coded invariants
carry no signal beyond first order that survives their own variance.

\subsection{Withdrawing that conclusion: it was coverage, not variance}
\label{sec:coverage}
The conclusion above is wrong, and the reason it is wrong is worth more than the
conclusion was. Every width-two configuration in that sweep took the top \(K\)
wires in index order and cut them into consecutive disjoint pairs, so each wire
appeared in exactly one table and the bank read \(K/2\) of the \(\binom{K}{2}\)
pairs available. At \(K=90\) that is 45 pairs out of 4005, one percent of the
lattice, and ``monotonically worse as \(K\) grows'' is what a fixed one-percent
sample looks like when the denominator grows. The experiment varied the wrong
quantity.

Replacing the single partition with \(R\) independent random partitions of all
wires --- every wire appears exactly once per round and meets different partners
each round --- makes the trend reverse. On the same held-out training half, and
with the scatter of the table outputs regularised by a ridge of \TabRidge{} of
its trace, the bank rises with \(R\) and passes the first-order functional. The
frozen configuration uses \TabRounds{} rounds over \NTabWires{} wires, giving
\NTabTables{} tables and \NTabCells{} cells of which \NTabCellsEmpty{}
(\TabCellsEmptyPct{}\%) are never addressed by any training residue, with
\TabCellOccupancy{} residues in the average occupied cell. It measures
\TabPickAuc{} on the held-out half against \PickLinDigits{} for the first-order
functional, and \TabAuc{} on the test fold.

Two further facts belong with it. The regulariser is load-bearing rather than
cosmetic: unregularised, the same solve measured 0.7844 at 1032 tables and 0.6846
at 1720, because random pairs repeat as the pool grows and the scatter goes
near-singular --- the same collinearity described in the note below, now appearing
as an instability in double precision rather than as round-off in single. And a purely
counting fusion, with multiplicities read off each table's compiled Gini index
instead of a solve, reaches only 0.6994 on this bank: with a thousand tables of
comparable individual informativeness, a rank statistic cannot tell which of them
are restating each other, and the ability to subtract is what the solve supplies.
An additive-only stagewise search, which can decline a redundant table but cannot
correct for one already taken, reaches 0.7687. We report these because they bound
how much of the result is counting and how much is the solve.

One methodological note belongs in the record. A first pass at this sweep
accumulated the \(1770\times1770\) Gram matrix in single precision and reported
that adding tables destroys the fit, collapsing from 0.74 to 0.60. Pair
fractions are strongly collinear --- two tables sharing a wire agree on a quarter
of their address --- so the Gram matrix is near-singular and single-precision
accumulation loses exactly the small eigenvalues that carry the interaction
signal. The collapse was round-off, not a property of the model class. We report
it because the failure mode is invisible in the output: the numbers are
plausible, ordered, and wrong.

\paragraph{Negative result: deformation does not help.} Both deformation
ablations score strictly below the rigid detector on all four metrics. The
anisotropic modes used here are global and low-frequency; they act as
macroscopic hinges that separate surface regions and create decoy volume, which
drags the predicted centre away from the true site. The correct next step
indicated by this failure is spatially gated, localized shear with rigid-hit
anchoring. We report this as a falsified hypothesis rather than removing the
ablations from the paper.

\section{Limitations}
The comparison covers one trained baseline, executed locally; PocketMiner could
not be included because per-residue predictions are not published. The 38
excluded multi-chain units mean the reported fold is a strict subset of the
official test fold, and the result does not transfer to multi-chain assemblies
without a chain-aware residue index.

Two of the four paired intervals between the table field and P2Rank contain
zero, which licenses ``we cannot separate these on this fold'' and not ``these
are equal''. All four point estimates favour the field, and on those two that is
not evidence of superiority: with \NUnits{} units the ROC-AUC interval is
roughly \(\pm0.02\) wide, so a margin of \TabAucD{} is exactly the size of
difference this fold cannot resolve. A reader who wants an ordering on ROC-AUC
or PR-AUC will not get one here and should not infer one from the sign.

Of the two intervals that do exclude zero, only one does so reliably. The F1
difference is \TabFOneD{} \TabFOneDCI{} and its interval clears zero under
\TabFOneSeedSig{} of \NSeedProbes{} independent resampling seeds. The MCC
difference is \TabMccD{} \TabMccDCI{} on \TabMccNPaired{} shared structures --- a
lower bound four ten-thousandths clear of zero, which survives \TabMccSeedSig{}
of \NSeedProbes{} seeds. A verdict that changes with the seed is a property of
the draw, so we report MCC as unresolved despite the frozen seed licensing the
opposite, and the counts are in
\texttt{results/official\_fold/SEED\_SENSITIVITY.json}. Even the surviving F1
result is a threshold metric at one operating point, against one trained
baseline executed locally, on one receptor-only task.

The test fold was read \NTestReads{} times over the development of the table
field itself, and that is not the whole story. The readings were \ReadAucI{},
\ReadAucII{} and \TabAuc{}, with paired differences against P2Rank of
\ReadDeltaI{}, \ReadDeltaII{} and \TabAucD{}. Each change between them was
motivated by a diagnosis made on the training fold --- an unregularised solve
that collapsed as the table pool grew, and a wire set whose ceiling the circuit
had already reached --- and each configuration was re-ranked on the held-out
training half before the fold was touched again. No reading was discarded.

The whole story is larger, and it is the most serious threat to everything
above. Counted from the artifacts rather than from memory,
\texttt{results/official\_fold/TEST\_FOLD\_ACCESS\_LEDGER.json} records
\NArchOnFold{} distinct architectures of ours scored on these same \NUnits{}
units: \NOurDetectors{} that reached the frozen telemetry and
\NStandaloneProbes{} standalone probes, one of which (\texttt{threshold\_field})
lands within four thousandths of the field reported here and appears in no table
in this paper. An earlier revision of this section said the fold had been read
three times, a figure typed from memory of the three probes that produced the
main result. The ledger is generated precisely because that is the sentence in
any paper least worth taking on trust, and it was wrong.

The arithmetic this forces should be done by us rather than left to the reader.
The standard error of a mean ROC-AUC over \NUnits{} units on this fold is
\FoldSE{}. The margin over P2Rank is \TabAucD{}, or \LeadInSE{} of one standard
error, and it is the maximum over \NArchOnFold{} architectures. The upward bias
of a maximum over that many correlated estimates is of the same order as the
margin itself, so the ROC-AUC ordering in Table~2 carries no weight and we ask
for none. That is why this paper claims parity: not modesty, but that the
number is the wrong size to claim with. The F1 difference is several times
larger and survives every resampling seed, which is what makes it the one
difference we report --- though seed robustness speaks to the bootstrap and not
to architecture selection, and a reader is entitled to discount it on exactly
the same grounds.

The absolute values remain modest --- ROC-AUC \TabAuc{} and F1 \TabFOne{} for
the strongest field here --- so the contribution is the construction rather than
the accuracy. What the construction buys is auditability and a mechanically
trivial inference path: every input is a closed-form algebraic, chemical or
counting function of the receptor; the compiled state is
\(\NTabCells{}\) pairs of integers plus \NTabTables{} integer multiplicities;
and scoring a residue is \NTabTables{} table reads, an integer sum, and one
spatial average. Whether that is worth anything depends on the application and
not on this measurement. No affinity, design or clinical claim follows from any
number in this paper.

\section{Reproducibility}
Every number above is regenerated from the repository by two commands: the
official-fold runner (fail-closed loader, per-file SHA-256 verification) and the
paired-bootstrap module (10{,}000 resamples, fixed seed). Parallelism across
structures is a scheduling change only and is order-preserving. Optional native
kernels are an operation-for-operation port of the reference implementation and
are covered by a bit-identity test, so enabling them cannot move a reported
value. Frozen artifacts and the telemetry schema are committed alongside the
code.

The environment is measured rather than declared. \texttt{ENVIRONMENT.json}
records the interpreter, the platform, the version of every library the numbers
depend on, the BLAS that NumPy actually bound to, and the P2Rank build together
with the JVM banner behind it; \texttt{requirements.txt} is generated from that
record, and a gate in \texttt{make verify} fails if the two disagree. This is not
bookkeeping for its own sake. An earlier revision of this repository declared a
NumPy pin that segfaults on import under the interpreter used here, so a reader
following the stated environment could not have started, let alone reproduced
anything; and the integer fan-out is obtained by rounding the solution of a
symmetric system, so a coefficient lying near a rounding boundary can land
differently under a different BLAS. The effect is small and bounded, and
recording which BLAS produced a table is cheaper than arguing about it.

The baseline is auditable without a JVM. For each of the \NUnits{} units,
P2Rank's own \texttt{*\_residues.csv} and \texttt{*\_predictions.csv} are
archived beside a \texttt{run.json} carrying the command, the P2Rank version,
the JVM banner and a SHA-256 of each file, all captured at run time. Every
number reported for P2Rank is a transformation of those two files, so a reader
can recompute the strongest competitor in the table from the archive alone. A
gate checks that it is the identity transformation where it should be: the
scores we evaluate must be P2Rank's own \texttt{probability} column and our
positive calls must be its own pocket assignment, unit by unit and value by
value. That gate exists because an earlier harness scored P2Rank from its top
five pocket centres with an invented distance cutoff and a reciprocal-rank
score, measured it at \(0.62\) against a published \(0.81\), and drew a
conclusion from the gap.

\subsection{An independent recomputation, and what it found}
Consistency gates compare artifacts to other artifacts. A mistake made before
the artifacts are written survives all of them, so the repository also carries a
recomputation that shares no code with the harness: it reads the committed
labels and per-residue scores, re-derives the four metrics and the paired
bootstrap from their definitions, and requires agreement to \(10^{-9}\) on 4608
per-unit values across the six residue-level detectors.

Writing it exposed a defect in residue identity. Identifiers were parsed by
taking their trailing digits and discarding the minus sign, so an
expression-tag residue numbered \(-1\) addressed the same slot as residue 1 and
the two exchanged scores: the tag residue inherited a real residue's ranking,
and the real residue -- which may be a labelled cryptic-site residue -- inherited
the tag's. Eleven of the \NUnits{} structures carry such a tag, one of them with
seven colliding pairs. A second copy of the rule, in the P2Rank adapter, failed
oppositely, dropping any row whose label carried an insertion code. Neither
raised; both moved published numbers.

Every detector was rescored on the official fold after the fix. The conclusions
are unchanged: 80 per-unit values moved, the largest by \(0.025\) ROC-AUC on a
single structure on which every method already scores below chance, and no fold
mean moved by more than \(1.6\times10^{-4}\). We report this rather than
silently re-freezing, because the informative part is not that the result
survived. It is that a genuine data-handling error was invisible to every check
that compared artifacts to artifacts and immediate to the one that compared them
to the data, which is an argument about what a reproducibility package should
contain, not about this paper.

Residue identity is now stated rather than implied. A residue is
\((\text{chain}, \text{resseq})\); the insertion code and the alternate-location
indicator are not part of it. That is forced by the label vocabulary --
CryptoBench marks a site with bare integers, so no label can separate 132 from
132A, and a finer key would populate the universe with residues no label could
ever mark positive. An audit records what follows: 47 tag residues across 15
units, five residues sharing a slot in the one structure with insertion codes,
77 units carrying alternate locations, and five labelled residues in four
structures, \(0.15\,\%\) of 3301, for which the apo crystal has no coordinates.
Those five lie outside every method's universe and are therefore neither hits
nor misses, so the recall reported here is over labelled residues \emph{present
in the apo structure}. The count is pinned in the audit, so a data refresh that
begins deleting positives fails rather than shrinking the denominator quietly.
